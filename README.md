# Rep & Ration

A diet and exercise website with recipes, workout plans and a daily tracker. It is one static file (`index.html`) and needs no server, build step or account.

## What's in it

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Calorie ring, protein/carb/fat progress, **quick food logging** right on the meals card (one search over whole foods, recipes and what you usually eat, one tap to add, portion steppers, and "same as yesterday"), a water glass that fills as you drink (+8, +16, +24 oz or 250/500/750 ml, or any amount) with a celebration when you hit your goal, this week's workouts, weight trend, next workout. Tap a highlighted day in the week strip to see that workout: the plan, time, and the weight and reps of every set. Confetti celebrations when you hit your water goal, your daily calories and protein, finish a workout or complete your weekly goal |
| **Recipes** | 47 recipes, grouped into Snacks, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Desserts with quick-jump links, with per-serving macros (including sugar), a servings scaler, step-by-step method and tips. Filter by meal or tag (high protein, sugar free, whole foods, minimally processed, gluten free, soy free, carnivore, vegetarian, vegan, low carb, meal prep, dessert), or search by ingredient. No refined sugar, honey or syrup anywhere: sweetness comes from fruit, pure monk fruit or a little coconut sugar. **Gluten free** and **soy free** are worked out from the ingredients and only include naturally free recipes (no processed gluten-free substitutes); each recipe lists what to buy certified or check the label for (oats, corn tortillas, broth, protein powder, dark chocolate). Each recipe has a drawn illustration you can replace with your own photo, and you can **add your own recipes** (ingredients, method, macros, tags) that work everywhere the built-in ones do. Tap the heart to save **favorites** and filter by them. Choose **Stovetop, Oven, Air fryer or Microwave** and every cooked recipe switches to written steps and times for that appliance; recipes an appliance doesn't suit say why and can be hidden |
| **Meal plan** | A month calendar (or a 7-day week view): tap a day to pick breakfast, lunch, dinner and a snack, with colored dots for each meal's main protein and daily calories against your target. Plan a week or the whole month at once: fill empty meals by Variety, Budget, Quick (30 min or less) or Batch cook, set servings for all meals, copy a day forward, and use "Same protein, less work" to switch lunches or dinners to one protein (rotating recipes or one bulk recipe) with the cost and ingredient savings shown. Undo, send the range to the shopping list, and log today's plan in one tap |
| **Shopping list** | Add recipes with the servings you need. Ingredients are merged and totaled across recipes, rounded up to whole items, grouped by store section, and can be ticked off or copied as text. You can also add your own items. **Stores & prices**: find grocery stores near a zip code or your location (OpenStreetMap), compare an estimated total for your list at each store, set a home store and favorites, swap items your home store probably doesn't carry, type real shelf prices to replace the estimates, and get **directions** (opens your maps app) or the store's **website**. US zip codes are looked up as US postal codes and the place found is shown. **Order online**: pick Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Kroger or Target, then copy your list and open the service in one tap, or search items one by one |
| **Workouts** | 28 plans from beginner to advanced, including six cardio plans (Zone 2 base building, rower intervals, bike sprints, treadmill incline walk & run, jump rope and a low-impact machine mix), plus a longevity strength and HIIT plan inspired by David Sinclair, an outdoor sprint, strength and ruck plan inspired by Paul Saladino, two Dorian Yates-style high-intensity bodybuilding splits (Blood & Guts one-set-to-failure, and his earlier two-set routine) and two Pavel Tsatsouline-style kettlebell programs (daily swings and get-ups, and clean-and-press ladders). 14 of them needing little or no equipment (bodyweight, one dumbbell, a resistance band, a pull-up bar, or three 30-minute kettlebell-only HIIT plans). Plans are sorted easiest to hardest (or the reverse) and can be filtered by difficulty and muscle group. Tell it what equipment you have, grouped into strength (dumbbells, kettlebell, bench, bands, pull-up bar, barbell, cables, leg machines) and cardio (treadmill, stationary bike, air bike, rowing machine, elliptical, stair climber, ski erg, jump rope) and it only shows plans you can do. Includes a live session mode with a rest timer where every set has its own weight and reps with big − / + buttons; each set starts from what you did last time, and a change carries forward to the following sets. **Create your own plans** with any number of days and exercises. **Workout reminders**: pick training days, a time and an alert, then add them to your phone or computer calendar as a recurring event; Today flags your training days. Favorite plans with the heart button and filter by them. Every plan shows an **estimated calories and fat burned** per session, personalized from your weight, height, sex and age (MET × Mifflin-St Jeor resting rate; fat shown as body-fat equivalent at 7.7 kcal/g). Live sessions, logged workouts and the weekly total on Today show it too |
| **Breathe** | 14 guided breathing exercises from around the world (box breathing, the physiological sigh, coherent breathing, 4-7-8, alternate-nostril, humming bee, Buteyko-style light breathing, resisted power breaths inspired by Bas Rutten, fighter's recovery breath, ocean breath, cooling breath, Zen breath counting, Tummo/Wim Hof-style power breathing with safety notes, and a long exhale for sleep). Each has step-by-step instructions, adjustable rounds and pace, and a full-screen guided timer: a softly glowing circle that grows and shrinks with your breath, a gentle tone at every change, optional vibration, and generated music |
| **Mind** | Daily brain habits from longevity research (a checklist with a streak), guided meditations (breath focus, body scan, loving-kindness, open awareness, walking, mental rehearsal, three good things), long-hold yin yoga sequences, and calisthenics holds for staying calm under strain, all with the same guided timer. Six relaxing tracks are synthesized in the browser (Still Water, Tide, Kalimba Drift, Singing Bowls, Low Pulse, Soft Rain) and can shuffle |
| **Daily log** | Add food (the same quick search as Today, grouped by meal with portion steppers), weight and water for any day. Shows a 30-day weight chart, 7-day calorie chart and workout history. Each workout expands to a full summary: time, sets, reps, weight lifted, calories, and every exercise set by set with personal bests marked |
| **Body & weight** | Body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arm, thigh, neck) with trend cards, an estimated body fat % (US Navy tape method), and progress photos with a side-by-side before/after view |
| **Charts & records** | Built from your workout log: workouts, active time, calories burned, total weight lifted and new personal bests for 1 week up to 1 year, all time or custom dates, with ‹ › to step back through earlier periods, compared with the period before. Weekly bars against your goal with a streak count. Per-exercise strength charts (weight used and estimated 1-rep max, or reps for bodyweight moves) and a personal records table. Cardio trends (session length and time spent working). Weight trend plus estimated lean vs fat mass from your measurements, to show muscle gain |
| **Profile & goals** | Calorie and macro calculator (Mifflin-St Jeor) using consensus targets: a deficit of at most 500 kcal or 20%, protein of 1.6–2.0 g/kg of a goal-range weight, fat at 30%, and a water goal from your weight and activity, with a "How these are set" note comparing the Saladino and Sinclair views. Goal setting, a **weekly training goal** (workouts and active minutes per week, shown on Today and in Charts & records), a default serving size (2 people unless changed) used by recipes, the shopping list and the meal planner, US/metric units, backup and restore, clear data |
| **Settings** | Color scheme (match device, light or dark), accent color, text size, celebrations on or off, units, and sound for breathing and mind sessions (music on or off, track or shuffle, music and tone volume, vibration). Every section is reached from a menu that opens when you tap the barbell logo |

## Added in the roadmap update

- **Your data is safe across updates.** Saved data is versioned and upgraded step by step, never reset. Unreadable data is kept aside instead of deleted.
- **Backups:** dated backup files, a preview before restoring (dates covered, meals, workouts, weigh-ins), and a reminder after 7 days.
- **Profiles:** two people can share one device. Each has their own name, units, targets, logs and photos.
- **Privacy:** a note in Settings explains what is stored and that it stays in this browser.
- **First run:** a 3-step setup (units, about you, goal) instead of example data. You can still look around with example data.
- **Faster logging:**
  - Edit any logged food, with Undo for adds, edits and deletes.
  - Mark favorite foods.
  - Barcode lookup through Open Food Facts.
  - Micronutrients (fiber, sodium, potassium, iron, B12) against FDA Daily Values.
- **Adaptive calorie target:** estimates your real daily burn from 4 weeks of logged intake and your weight trend, then suggests a new target.
- **Shopping:**
  - One line per ingredient even across units.
  - "Buy 2 × 15 oz can" package hints.
  - A "have it" pantry list.
  - Build the list from this week's meal plan.
  - Share, email, text or print.
- **Recipes (52):**
  - Allergens (the FDA's nine), estimated cost per serving, equipment and storage.
  - Vegetarian and lower-carb swaps.
  - Your own star rating and notes.
  - Dairy free, Nut free and Budget filters.
  - Nutrition checked against USDA-based ingredient values: see `docs/recipe-macro-audit.md` and `tools/nutrition_audit.py`.
- **Workouts (33 plans):**
  - An exercise library (`#exercises`) linked from every plan, with form cues, muscles, equipment, easier and harder versions, and your records.
  - In sessions: next-step progression suggestions, a plate calculator, effort (RPE), personal-best alerts and supersets.
  - Share any plan as a link.
- **Breathe & Mind:**
  - Three Wim Hof-style exercises with holds you end yourself.
  - A 7-day "Learn to breathe" course.
  - Sleep sounds with a timer and two sleep wind-downs.
- **Mobile app** (`mobile/`): an Expo + TypeScript app with Today, Log and Targets, backup import, and Supabase accounts with row-level security. See `mobile/README.md` and `docs/HEALTH.md`.

## Use it on your phone

Open https://xsosaxdex.github.io/Ray/ on your phone, then add it to your home screen. It gets its own icon, opens full-screen without the browser bar, and works offline once it has been opened.

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮**, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

Your entries stay on that phone. To move them from another device, use a backup (see Data below).

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://localhost:8000.

## Testing

Serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server 8765`), then run `node tests/smoke.js` (needs Playwright) and `node tests/library.js`. Mobile: `cd mobile && npm test`. It opens every page at phone and desktop width with fresh, old-format and damaged saved data, and fails if any page is blank, shows an error, or scrolls sideways.

## Deploy

Upload `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and the `icons/` folder to any static host, such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel or Cloudflare Pages.

## Data

Each visitor's entries are saved in their own browser (`localStorage` key `rep-ration-v1`). Progress photos and recipe photos are shrunk to at most 1200px and stored in IndexedDB (`rep-ration-photos`), because they're too large for localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server, so data does not sync between devices. To move a log to another browser, use **Targets → Download backup**, then **Restore from backup** on the other device. Backups contain everything except photos. On the first visit the site shows clearly labeled example data, which can be cleared with one click.

## Editing content

Recipes and workout plans are plain arrays at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html` (`RECIPES` and `PLANS`). To add one, copy an existing entry and change it. Macros are per serving. A recipe can take an optional `photo` URL, which replaces its illustration (users can also add their own recipes and plans from the site).

The diet tags follow simple rules, explained on the Recipes page when a filter is selected:

- **Sugar free**: no sugar, honey, syrups, sweeteners or fruit; 8 g or less of naturally occurring sugar per serving.
- **Whole foods**: only unprocessed, single-ingredient foods. Give these recipes the Minimally processed tag as well.
- **Minimally processed**: whole foods plus a few clean staples (canned beans, tuna and tomatoes, bone broth or stock, full-fat coconut milk, 3-ingredient corn tortillas, Dijon, unflavored grass-fed whey or collagen).

Ingredient standards for every built-in recipe: no seed oils or cooking sprays (olive oil, avocado oil, butter or ghee instead), full-fat dairy, whole eggs rather than carton egg whites, arrowroot instead of cornstarch, coconut aminos instead of soy sauce, protein powder only as unflavored whey or collagen without sweeteners or gums, and raw honey or pure maple syrup in small amounts.
